The Process

Steeped. Dried. Rolled.

Every blend starts as loose whole-leaf tea from small farms. We steep it to draw the tannin down, dry it slow over three days in our Gowanus workshop, then roll it by hand in unbleached botanical paper. No additives, no accelerants, no filters dipped in anything.

Each pack holds ten cigarettes. Each cigarette burns about eight minutes — roughly the length of one good record side break, which is the point.
